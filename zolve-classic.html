The details

New to the US? This card was built for exactly that.

Credit invisibility hits newcomers twice: no history means no card, and no card means no history. Zolve was built to break that loop for people new to the US — including applicants with only a passport.

The design is simple: your credit line is tied to the balance in your Zolve checking account. No US history required to start.

How it works

Open a Zolve account (passport or visa documents accepted), fund it, and your card limit follows your balance. Payments report to all 3 bureaus. No annual fee; the costs to watch are the 3% FX fee on non-USD transactions and APR 19.5–39.5% if you carry a balance.

Who it's for — and who should look elsewhere

Good fit if you:
  • New to the US — even passport-only, before SSN/ITIN
  • Want a real unsecured card without deposit
  • Building from absolute zero
Look elsewhere if you:
  • Have an established US file (better unsecured options exist)
  • Want zero FX fees on foreign spending
  • Carry balances monthly (APR is high — pay in full)

How it compares

Versus the Amex-via-Nova-Credit path: Amex needs a credit history in one of 12 covered countries plus SSN/ITIN to issue — Zolve works from nothing. Zolve is the only mainstream card for passport-only applicants.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does this card hurt my credit score?
Designed for no-history applicants — there is nothing to check yet. Payments report positively from the start.
What credit score do I need?
None — no US history required. Passport/visa documents accepted for identity.
How long does it take to build credit with this card?
Reporting starts with the first cycle; most newcomers see a first score around month 6 (FICO requires 6 months of reported history).
What happens to my deposit?
No deposit — your line is tied to your Zolve checking balance.
Can I be denied?
Yes — identity verification or account funding issues. Credit history itself is not the barrier.
