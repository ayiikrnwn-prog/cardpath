The details

No deposit. No fees. Underwritten by your cash flow, not your score.

Traditional underwriting asks what your score says. Petal asks what your money actually does — banking cash-flow decides approval, which changes who gets yes.

For people with a thin file but steady money movement, Petal 2 is the strongest no-deposit no-fee unsecured option — with one hard requirement: an SSN.

How it works

Apply connecting your bank account; Petal analyzes income and spending patterns instead of relying on score history. Approved limits typically start $500-1,500+. No fees at all. Payments report to all 3 bureaus.

Who it's for — and who should look elsewhere

Good fit if you:
  • Thin file but regular income landing in a bank
  • SSN holder (hard requirement)
  • Want unsecured with zero fees
Look elsewhere if you:
  • ITIN-only or passport-only (see Zolve)
  • No US bank account activity to analyze
  • Active bankruptcy

How it compares

Versus Credit One (the other no-deposit pick): Petal has $0 fees versus $75-99/year, and cash-flow underwriting is friendlier to thin files. Petal wins wherever SSN exists. Versus Varo Believe: Varo is spend-only — Petal is a real credit line.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does this card hurt my credit score?
Application uses a soft pull for pre-qualification; accepting the offer may involve a hard inquiry.
What credit score do I need?
No minimum — cash-flow underwriting means banking history can substitute for score.
How long does it take to build credit with this card?
First-cycle reporting; movement typically within 3–6 months.
What happens to my deposit?
No deposit — unsecured.
Can I be denied?
Yes — mainly if banking history shows insufficient or irregular income. SSN is required.
