The details

Build credit with a loan — and get the money back.

Credit-builder loans are the underrated sibling of secured cards: no deposit upfront, no card to misuse — just fixed monthly payments into savings that get reported as installment history.

Credit Strong is the strongest option in the category after Self: plans from $15/month, principal returned at completion, plans scaling to $25,000.

How it works

Pick a plan; Credit Strong places the loan amount in a locked savings account. Monthly payments report to all 3 bureaus as installment history. At completion, the savings are yours. No credit check.

Who it's for — and who should look elsewhere

Good fit if you:
  • No deposit for a secured card
  • Want installment history (mix = 10% of score)
  • Saving discipline alongside credit building
Look elsewhere if you:
  • Need spending power now (cards give a line immediately)
  • Want the absolute cheapest (Kikoff is $5 but no money back)

How it compares

Versus Self: Self doubles as a secured card once savings progress (hybrid); Credit Strong is loan-only with longer/bigger plans. Versus Kikoff: Kikoff is $5 but nothing comes back — Credit Strong returns your principal.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does this loan hurt my credit score?
No credit check to open. Missed payments would be reported — autopay recommended.
What credit score do I need?
None — built for no-score and rebuilding profiles.
How long does it take to build credit with this loan?
Installment history reports monthly; CFPB data shows builder loans meaningfully raise score odds for thin files. Movement in 3–6 months.
What happens to my deposit?
It is your own savings — returned at plan completion, minus fees.
Can I be denied?
Rarely — mainly identity verification issues.
