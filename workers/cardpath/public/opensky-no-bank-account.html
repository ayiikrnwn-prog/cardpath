The details

No bank account? No problem — that's the point.


An estimated 4.5% of US households are unbanked — no checking account, no direct deposit, no relationship with a bank. Nearly every credit builder card quietly assumes you have one. OpenSky does not.

Whether you left traditional banking by choice or circumstance, OpenSky lets you fund a refundable deposit by money order, debit card, or bank transfer — no account required to apply or to keep the card.

How it works

Choose a deposit between $100 and $3,000 — it becomes your credit line and is fully refundable. No credit check, no bank account, no Chime-style direct deposit requirements. Payments report to all three bureaus. Manage the account online or by phone; the $35 annual fee covers the flexibility.

Who it's for — and who should look elsewhere

Good fit if you:
  • No checking account, by choice or circumstance
  • Prefer not to link bank credentials anywhere
  • Rebuilding with cash-based finances
Look elsewhere if you:
  • Want a $0-fee option and have a bank account (Chime)
  • Want rewards (Quicksilver Secured)
  • Have $0 to deposit (Self builds via loan instead)

FAQ

Frequently asked questions


Does checking or applying hurt my credit score?
No credit check is performed — application does not touch your score.
What credit score do I need?
None. Deposit and identity verification decide approval.
How long does it take to build credit with this card?
First-cycle reporting; typical builders see movement within 3–6 months of on-time payments.
What happens to my deposit?
Refundable at closure in good standing or upgrade.
Can I be denied?
Yes, mainly for identity or funding verification issues — not for score or banking history.
