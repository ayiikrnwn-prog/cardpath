The details

Your biggest bill, finally counted: report your rent.

Renters pay the biggest bill of their life every month and get zero credit for it — literally. BoomPay fixes that: your rent, reported to all 3 bureaus, for $2 a month.

The killer feature is back-reporting: up to 24 months of past rent can be added, so your file starts with history instead of from zero.

How it works

Connect your bank or landlord; Boom verifies rent payments and reports them to Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion. $2/month covers ongoing reporting; back-reporting adds past history immediately. No credit check.

Who it's for — and who should look elsewhere

How it compares

Versus Experian Boost (free): Boost is free but Experian-only and skips rent. Boom is $2 but 3-bureau rent reporting with backdating. For renters the combo of both is the strongest free-ish start available.
