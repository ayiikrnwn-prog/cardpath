The details

A real card from a major bank — for the price of a pizza night.


Getting rejected because of a thin credit file stings twice: once by the denial, and again by every bank that sees it afterward. Capital One built the Platinum Secured precisely for that moment — for people who are new to credit or rebuilding after mistakes, and who cannot put hundreds of dollars on the table to prove themselves.

Most secured cards want $200 or more upfront. Capital One lowers that bar: your initial credit line can start at $49, $99, or $200 depending on your approval — you find out the exact amount before you commit a cent. No annual fee means the card costs you nothing while it quietly builds your file.

How it works

The mechanics are simple. You place a refundable security deposit with Capital One — that deposit becomes your credit line, not a fee. You use the card for small purchases, pay on time, and Capital One reports those payments to all three bureaus. After your first six months, Capital One reviews your account and may raise your credit line without an additional deposit. Responsible use over time can lead to graduation to an unsecured card and your deposit returned.

Who it's for — and who should look elsewhere

Good fit if you:
  • New to credit with no score yet
  • Rebuilding after past mistakes
  • Can put aside $49–$200 as a one-time deposit
  • Want a major-bank card, not a subprime issuer
Look elsewhere if you:
  • You need rewards from day one (look at the Quicksilver Secured)
  • You have no bank account at all (OpenSky works without one)
  • You want a card today with zero deposit (Credit One, with its annual fee)

FAQ

Frequently asked questions


Does checking or applying hurt my credit score?
There is no hard credit pull to find out your pre-qualification. A full application does involve a credit check by the issuer, but for a secured card approval odds are not score-driven the way unsecured cards are.
What credit score do I need?
Capital One does not publish a minimum score for this card — it is designed for people with no score, limited history, or rebuilding credit. Your deposit and ability to fund it matter more than your score.
How long does it take to build credit with this card?
Payments are reported to all three bureaus from your first billing cycle. Many builders see measurable score movement after 3–6 months of on-time reporting; Capital One reviews your line for increase after 6 months.
What happens to my deposit?
It stays in a secured account and becomes your credit line — it is not a fee. If you upgrade to unsecured or close the account in good standing, the deposit is refunded.
Can I be denied?
Yes — secured does not mean guaranteed. Common denial reasons: insufficient income, unresolved delinquencies, or too many recent applications. You keep your deposit if you are denied before funding.
