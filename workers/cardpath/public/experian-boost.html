The details

Free. Instant. The easiest points you will ever add.

Before you apply for anything: check if you can add points for free in five minutes. Experian Boost connects your bank account, finds your phone/utility/streaming payments, and adds them to your Experian file — average +13 FICO 8 points, at $0.

Sign up free, connect your bank, select the bills you want counted. Boost applies them to your Experian file instantly. Go builds a fresh file if you have none. The honest limit: it only affects Experian.
