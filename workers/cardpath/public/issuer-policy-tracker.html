Methodology — how we verify

Every row on this page is checked against the issuer’s own application page, disclosure, or help center — never a third-party summary, never a blog post. Each row carries the month of verification; rows older than 90 days are treated as stale and re-checked at the next quarterly pass. When two sources conflict, the issuer’s own page wins and the discrepancy gets a note. Corrections from readers: our contact page promises a fix within 5 business days (editorial policy), and the correction date is appended to the row — corrections are evidence of a working process, not something to hide.

Why this matters commercially as well as editorially: stale policy claims are the most common factual error across comparison sites, and the most expensive for the reader — an applicant who trusts a dead graduation policy or an outdated ITIN rule loses a hard inquiry and months of timing. Dated verification is the cheapest trust signal a finance site can build.
