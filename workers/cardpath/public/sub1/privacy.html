Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026 · CCPA/CPRA compliant

What we collect

Quiz answers (score situation, deposit budget, goals) — stored as anonymous bands, never tied to your name. Email address — only if you opt in, collected for the match report and email course, with double opt-in confirmation. Usage events — page views and interactions, tied to an anonymous ID, used to improve the quiz. Cookies/advertising — advertising partners (Google Ad Manager) may set cookies subject to your consent choices in our cookie banner.

What we never do

We never sell your personal information. We never share your email with issuers — applying happens on the issuer's site, under their privacy policy. We never put your answers in URLs in a form that identifies you.

Your rights (CCPA/CPRA)

You may request: what we collect, deletion of your data, correction, and opt-out of sale/sharing (we don't sell — requests still honored). Email privacy@cardpath.example or use the contact form. We respond within 45 days.

Email list

Double opt-in: after you enter your email, we send a confirmation first — you're subscribed only after you confirm. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe. Unsubscribing removes you from sequences but not from your quiz result page.

Advertising partners

We use Google Ad Manager. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites. You can opt out of personalized advertising at google.com/settings/ads or via our consent banner (TCF-compliant).
