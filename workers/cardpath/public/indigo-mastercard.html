The details

See your odds before you apply — no hard pull.


The scariest part of rebuilding is not denial — it is another hard inquiry on a file that cannot afford one. Indigo's pre-qualification answers the question first: see whether you are likely approved, with no score impact, before you decide.

For people who have been burned by repeated applications, that order of operations — check, then apply — is the whole value proposition.

How it works

Start with pre-qualification: a soft check that does not affect your score. If the odds look good, complete the application (that step does involve a hard pull). The card is unsecured with no deposit; annual fee depends on the offer you are pre-qualified for — some are $0 the first year, others carry $75+ thereafter. Payments report to all three bureaus.

Who it's for — and who should look elsewhere

Good fit if you:
  • Want to test approval odds without a hard inquiry
  • Fair or rebuilding credit with some history
  • Prefer unsecured over deposit
Look elsewhere if you:
  • Can fund a deposit — $0-fee secured cards are cheaper over time
  • No score at all (no-credit-check secured cards fit better)
  • Want rewards (Quicksilver Secured)

FAQ

Frequently asked questions


Does checking or applying hurt my credit score?
Pre-qualification is a soft pull — no impact. Completing a full application does involve a hard inquiry.
What credit score do I need?
Designed for fair to rebuilding credit; the pre-qualification tells you where you stand before applying.
How long does it take to build credit with this card?
Reporting starts with the first cycle; typical movement within 3–6 months of on-time payments.
What happens to my deposit?
No deposit — this card is unsecured. Costs come from the annual fee structure of your offer.
Can I be denied?
Yes, even after pre-qualification — pre-qual is directional, not a guarantee. Final approval depends on the full underwriting check.
