The details

Your home-country credit history can follow you here.

Myth-busting first, because almost every comparison site gets this wrong: Nova Credit does NOT work with Petal, Zolve, OpenSky, or Self. As of 2026, only Chase (Canada/UK history) and American Express (12 countries) have live pathways — verified against the Nova Credit tracker.

If your history is in a covered country and you have SSN/ITIN, Amex-via-Nova can approve you with your foreign file instead of an empty US one.

How it works

Apply through the Amex-Nova flow; consent to pulling your foreign credit file; if approved, you get a real Amex card reporting to US bureaus. You still need SSN or ITIN to issue — passport-only is Zolve territory, not this.

Who it's for — and who should look elsewhere

Good fit if you:
  • Newcomers WITH SSN/ITIN + history in a covered country
  • Want a premium card immediately
Look elsewhere if you:
  • Passport-only, no SSN/ITIN yet (Zolve first)
  • History not in the 12 countries
  • No foreign file to pull

How it compares

Versus Zolve: Zolve works from zero with passport-only; Amex-Nova needs existing foreign history + SSN/ITIN — but delivers a premium card instead of an entry-level one. They are different stages of the same journey.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does this hurt my credit score?
The application involves a hard pull on your US file (if any) and a check of your foreign file via Nova.
What credit score do I need?
No US score required — foreign history substitutes. SSN or ITIN is required to issue.
How long does it take to build credit with this card?
US reporting starts with the first cycle — your premium card builds US history from day one.
What happens to my deposit?
No deposit — unsecured.
Can I be denied?
Yes — country not covered, insufficient foreign history, or identity verification.
