The details

The $5/month option — honest about the trade.

When the budget is absolutely fixed, every dollar matters. Kikoff is the floor of the market: $5 a month, reported to Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion, no credit check.

We show the catch on the same screen as the price: unlike Self or Credit Strong, Kikoff payments are a fee — the money does not come back.

How it works

Subscribe, and Kikoff reports a tradeline to all 3 bureaus monthly. No deposit, nothing to manage beyond the $5 — set it and let history accumulate.

Who it's for — and who should look elsewhere

Good fit if you:
  • Fixed tight budget ($5 is the whole commitment)
  • Want all-3-bureau reporting without a deposit
Look elsewhere if you:
  • Want money back at the end (Credit Strong/Self return principal)
  • Want spending power (this is not a card)
  • Can afford $15+/mo for a plan that returns your money

How it compares

Versus Credit Strong ($15): Credit Strong costs 3x but returns principal — over 12 months that is $180 back versus $60 gone. Kikoff wins only when $5 is genuinely the ceiling.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does this account hurt my credit score?
No credit check. Payments report positively; missed payments would report negatively.
What credit score do I need?
None.
How long does it take to build credit with this account?
Monthly reporting from the start; movement typically in 3–6 months.
What happens to my deposit?
No deposit and no refund — the $5/month is a fee. That is the trade for the lowest entry price.
Can I be denied?
Rarely — identity verification only.
