The details

The secured card that graduates you — automatically.


The dirty secret of secured cards: many issuers are happy to keep your deposit forever. Discover designed the it® Secured to end — automatic reviews begin at 7 months, and cardholders in good standing graduate to unsecured with their deposit refunded.

That graduation path changes the psychology of rebuilding. You are not renting a credit line; you are on a timed track out of it.

How it works

Fund a $200 minimum refundable deposit. Spend and pay on time — Discover reports to all three bureaus. You earn 2% cash back at gas stations and restaurants (on up to $1,000 in combined purchases per quarter) and 1% on everything else, with all cash back matched at the end of your first year. Starting at 7 months, Discover reviews your account automatically; graduation depends on on-time payments and spending, not a score cutoff you have to guess.

Who it's for — and who should look elsewhere

Good fit if you:
  • Rebuilding with steady income and on-time habits
  • Can fund $200 and keep utilization low
  • Want the clearest, most automatic graduation path
Look elsewhere if you:
  • Cannot fund $200 yet (Capital One from $49)
  • No bank account (OpenSky)
  • Active bankruptcy (OpenSky is more forgiving)

FAQ

Frequently asked questions


Does checking or applying hurt my credit score?
Pre-qualification is a soft check. Applying triggers a hard inquiry, but Discover evaluates secured applications with rebuilding applicants in mind rather than score-minimums.
What credit score do I need?
No published minimum. This card accepts limited, fair, and rebuilding credit — the deposit and income matter most.
How long does it take to build credit with this card?
Reporting begins with the first cycle; many see score movement in 3–6 months. Graduation reviews start at 7 months and continue periodically.
What happens to my deposit?
Refunded when you graduate to unsecured or close in good standing — that is the design goal of the product.
Can I be denied?
Yes — especially with unresolved delinquencies or excessive recent applications. Discover offers pre-qualification to check first.
